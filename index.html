Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Buch Athletics ("we", "us") collects and uses the personal information you share with us. That includes forms on Facebook and Instagram, WhatsApp messages, phone calls, and visits to our club at Buch Commercial, Buch Executive Villas, Bosan Road, Multan, Pakistan.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and, if you provide it, your email address.
  • Your answers to our questions: for example which facility interests you (gym, pool or classes), when you would like to start, which membership plan you are considering, the area you live in, and the best time to call you.
  • Messages: what you send us on WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram.
  • Membership information if you join, such as your plan, payments and attendance.

We do not ask for passwords, card details or national ID numbers through online forms.

2. How we use it

  • To contact you by phone or WhatsApp about the membership, class or facility you asked about.
  • To arrange a visit or tour of the club, and to share prices and offers you requested.
  • To manage your membership if you join.
  • To improve our services and understand which of our ads and offers are useful to people.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With our own staff who need it to reply to you or manage your membership.
  • With service providers we use to run our business, such as Meta (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp), which processes the forms and messages you send us.
  • When the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

If you do not become a member, we keep your enquiry for up to 12 months and then delete it. If you become a member, we keep your information for as long as your membership is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records.

5. Your choices

  • You can ask us to stop calling or messaging you at any time. Reply "STOP" on WhatsApp or tell us on a call.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

6. Security

We limit access to your information to the staff who need it and take reasonable steps to keep it safe. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, but we work to protect it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
